The story behind Family Tree

Families scatter. Cousins move to different cities, aunts emigrate, grandparents pass on, and the stories that once bound everyone together start to fade. A family tree is the simplest, oldest way to keep those connections alive — to see at a glance who's related to whom, where everyone came from, and how the branches grew.

This app was built so that every family member can contribute. Not just the one tech-savvy cousin who maintains a spreadsheet — anyone with a phone can add themselves, add their parents, upload a photo, pin their birthplace on the map. The tree grows with every person who touches it. Years from now, the next generation will open it and see the whole family, mapped out by the people who lived it.

Questions, answered

Is Family Tree free to use?

Yes, completely free. The app runs on Supabase's free tier and is hosted on Vercel's free plan. There are no subscriptions, no ads, no in-app purchases. It was built to be shared.

Is my family's data private?

Absolutely. Only people with your family's 6-character share code can see or edit your tree. The code is not searchable, not indexed by Google, and not shared with anyone. Each family is isolated — your tree is invisible to other families using the app.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The app runs in any web browser. On iPhone, you can "Add to Home Screen" in Safari to get an app icon. On Android, you can download the APK from GitHub Releases for a native install. Or just open it in Chrome — it works the same.

What if I don't know someone's birth year?

Leave it blank. Birth year is optional — many older relatives' exact birth years aren't known, and that shouldn't stop you from adding them to the tree. You can always fill it in later when someone remembers.

Can multiple family members edit at the same time?

Yes. The app uses real-time sync via Supabase, so when your cousin in Dubai adds a person, you in Kannur see it within seconds. Everyone with the share code has full edit access — it's a shared family document.

What happens to the data if Supabase pauses?

Supabase's free tier pauses projects after 7 days of inactivity. We have a GitHub Actions cron job that pings the database every 6 hours to keep it alive. Even if it does pause, your data is preserved — just log into the Supabase dashboard and click "Restore" to resume.

Can I export the tree?

Yes. From the app's menu (the ⋯ button in the top-right), you can export the current tree view as a PNG image or a PDF document. Perfect for printing and sharing.
